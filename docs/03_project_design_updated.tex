\section{Introduction}
% ===================================================================

This chapter presents the \textbf{updated} project design of the
\textit{AI Medical Pre-Screening Assistant}.  The original CSE499A
design was a five-stage, one-shot pipeline: Patient Speech $\to$ ASR
$\to$ Medical NER $\to$ Structured EHR $\to$ Doctor Dashboard.
Empirical findings from Phases~2 and~3 revealed that this linear
approach was insufficient: (i)~patients frequently omit critical
clinical information in a single recording, (ii)~there was no mechanism
to handle life-threatening emergencies, and (iii)~the system provided
no explanation of its outputs. The design has therefore been expanded
to \textbf{15 functional modules} that add an adaptive conversational
follow-up loop, emergency detection, risk classification, explainable
AI, and a doctor feedback and retraining pipeline. The system
remains strictly a \textit{pre-screening and decision-support tool};
all final clinical authority rests with the attending physician.

\subsection{Module Descriptions}
% ===================================================================

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0pt,labelwidth=0pt,style=nextline,
                    itemsep=2pt,parsep=0pt]

\item[\textbf{M1 --- Speech-to-Text.}]
Accepts Bangla, Banglish, and regional dialect speech (Dhakaiya,
Barishali, Sylheti). VAD-segments audio into 10--15\,s clips.
Best benchmark: \textit{BengaliAI Regional} (46.94\% WER); target
after CSE499B LoRA fine-tuning: $<$20\% WER.

\item[\textbf{M2 --- Text Processing \& Normalisation.}]
Spelling correction, Banglish normalisation, filler-word removal,
punctuation restoration, sentence-boundary detection. Bilingual
Bangla--English throughout.

\item[\textbf{M3 --- Information Extraction (NER).}]
BanglaBERT token classifier labels: \textit{symptom, body site,
duration, severity, frequency, age, comorbidity, medication}.
Temporal expressions normalised to standard intervals.

\item[\textbf{M4 --- Initial Clinical Summary.}]
Concise human-readable chief-complaint summary used downstream and
displayed on the dashboard.
Example: \textit{``Fever + dry cough for 3 days; mild fatigue; no chest pain.''}

\item[\textbf{M5 --- Emergency Detection} \textcolor{cred}{(new).}]
Rule-based critical-symptom checker + AI classifier.
Red flags: chest pain, stroke signs, severe dyspnoea, loss of
consciousness, acute trauma. Escalates immediately; pipeline continues
in parallel for non-critical cases.

\item[\textbf{M6 --- Missing Information Analysis} \textcolor{cred}{(new).}]
Compares extracted entities against a per-symptom clinical checklist
(\CheckedBox\ Fever detected; \XBox\ Duration missing; \XBox\
Temperature not provided) and outputs a structured gap report.

\item[\textbf{M7 --- Follow-up Question Generation} \textcolor{corange}{(new).}]
Generates priority-ranked questions in Bangla or English.
Example: \textit{``Apnar jwor ki matro kal theke?''} / \textit{``When did the
fever start?''} Already-answered items are never repeated.

\item[\textbf{M8 --- Response Processing \& Profile Update} \textcolor{corange}{(new).}]
Patient answers re-enter M2--M3. Extracted entities merge into the
profile with conflict resolution; filled gaps are marked.

\item[\textbf{M9 --- Case Completion Check} \textcolor{corange}{(new).}]
Scores profile completeness against a configurable threshold.
If incomplete $\to$ loops to M7; if complete (or max turns reached)
$\to$ proceeds to M10.

\item[\textbf{M10 --- Risk Assessment} \textcolor{cred}{(new).}]
Assigns \textbf{Low / Medium / High / Critical} using a clinical rule
engine weighted by age, comorbidities, severity, and duration.
Integrates M5 output.

\item[\textbf{M11 --- Explainable AI (XAI)} \textcolor{cred}{(new).}]
SHAP/LIME attribution converted to plain language.
Example: \textit{``High risk: fever $>$5 days + dyspnoea + age $>$65.''}
Displayed alongside every risk classification.

\item[\textbf{M12 --- Clinical Report Generation} \textcolor{cred}{(new).}]
8-section pre-screening report: Patient Profile, Chief Complaint,
Symptoms, Emergency Flags, Q\&A Log, Risk + XAI, Condition
Categories, Next Steps. Exported as PDF and to the dashboard.

\item[\textbf{M13 --- EHR Database.}]
HIPAA/PDPA-compliant PostgreSQL with encryption at rest and in
transit. Full audit logging; efficient retrieval by patient ID or date.

\item[\textbf{M14 --- Doctor Dashboard} (extended from original).]
Displays report, risk tier, emergency flags, and XAI explanation.
Annotation and override of any field (all changes versioned); real-time
alerts for High/Critical cases.

\item[\textbf{M15 --- Feedback \& Continuous Learning} \textcolor{cgreen}{(new).}]
Doctors rate reports, correct extractions, flag missed items, annotate
risk disagreements. Periodic retraining of M3, M7, M10 with regression
testing before deployment.

\end{description}

\section{Conclusion}
% ===================================================================

The updated 15-module design addresses every limitation of the original
five-stage pipeline. The adaptive follow-up loop (Modules~6--9) ensures
complete clinical profiles before assessment. Module~5 adds an
emergency escalation pathway that was entirely absent before.
Modules~10--11 introduce risk stratification and transparency through
explainable AI. Module~15 closes the quality loop by feeding physician
corrections back into model retraining. The CSE499B priority is LoRA
fine-tuning of Qwen3-ASR-1.7B to reduce dialect WER to $<$20\%;
once the ASR foundation is reliable, Modules~3--15 will be built
and integrated iteratively.
