---

## 📋 HOW TO UPDATE THIS FILE

After every session (takes 2 minutes):
1. Add **one row** to the Session History Table below
2. Paste the **SESSION SUMMARY block** from `chatlog.md` into the Session Summaries section
3. Update the **Quick Stats** numbers
4. Update the **Streak Tracker** for today
5. If something clicked in a big way, add it to **Memorable Moments**
6. Every Sunday, add a **Weekly Review** entry

**Ask your AI to help:**
> *"Give me a 1-line summary of today's session for my historychat.md table. Include: session number, date, project, what I did, and the key thing I learned. Max 15 words for the summary column."*
